04 / Story

Quest log

From a handful of veterans to a 100-strong studio with 12 million players.

2020
The party forms
  • A small team of veterans with years of mid-core and casual shipping experience comes together.
  • First project Broyalty - strong retention, but short of expectations. Moves to maintenance; the lessons stay.
2022
TOP APP GAMES is founded
  • Development begins on Ludus: Merge Arena - a merge-battler built around deep card mechanics and endlessly repeatable gameplay.
2023
Soft launch
  • Ludus ships its first version to exceptional early retention, validating the core loop and setting up the global rollout.
2024
Worldwide launch
  • 1 million players within months of global launch.
  • Utmost Games - the holding founded by former MY.GAMES CEO Vladimir Nikolsky - invests, calling the studio a future billion-dollar business.
  • Vladimir Markov, creator of Hustle Castle (80M players), is appointed CEO.
  • Year closes at 3M downloads, $2M+ monthly revenue and a fresh $10M funding round.
2025
Going big on partnerships
  • Ludus grows to 8 million players.
  • Crossover with Warcrow, the tabletop franchise by Corvus Belli, brings legendary heroes into the arena.
  • UFC heavyweight Alexander "Drago" Volkov joins as a playable hero - timed to UFC 321.
  • Global Creator Program launches; distribution expands with Aptoide.
2026
12 million and counting
  • Ludus passes 12 million players, with a roster of 100+ heroes.
  • The team keeps scaling, with new projects in production.
